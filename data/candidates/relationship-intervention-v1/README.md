# Relationship + Intervention V1 candidate workspace

This directory is a non-governed research workspace. Its records are excluded
from the production graph and may never be treated as canonical merely because
they are committed to the repository.

Every record placed here must remain in a non-governed lifecycle state with
`activationStatus: NOT_ELIGIBLE`. Promotion requires an external, authorized
human governance decision and deterministic materialization through the V1
governance validator. The workspace is intentionally empty at infrastructure
launch; Family audits and scientific population are not authorized.
