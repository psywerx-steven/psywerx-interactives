# Governed native Actions & Events store

The catalog contains the exact INF-F03 records approved by governance decision
`GOV-INF-F03-001-2026-09-06`: six reusable HappeningType identities, two scoped
EffectAssertions, and two EvidenceAssessments. Every native Actions & Events
record is governed. Partial activation decision 001 activates only
`HT-V1-INF-F03-002`, `EA-V1-INF-F03-002`, and its EvidenceAssessment. The other
seven records remain `GOVERNED + INACTIVE`. Scientific activation does not grant
model eligibility or practitioner-action eligibility.

Existing RI scientific records remain authoritative in their original stores and
are accessible through SAME_IDENTITY read views only. No model/recommendation
execution or new application consumer is present.
See [production contract documentation](../../docs/ACTIONS_EVENTS_V1.md).
