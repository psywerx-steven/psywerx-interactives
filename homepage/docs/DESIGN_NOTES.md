# Approved design direction implemented

## Hierarchy

1. White header with the supplied compact PSYWERX wordmark.
2. Navy hero with the sole approved brand line, the nonprofit mission, and practical entry actions.
3. Two live products: Driver Taxonomy Explorer and Cognitive Security Explorer.
4. Six expandable platform areas, in the approved order.
5. Interactive knowledge architecture overview.
6. Training & Education, explicitly Coming soon.
7. Early mission expanded in an About section.
8. Quiet newsletter and LinkedIn footer.

The research rail sits to the right of the main column on desktop and follows the main content on smaller screens. It is a readable scrolling list, not an animated ticker. It shows only owner-published Morning Brief items. The page does not fetch private documents or expose the four substantive research-note sections.

## Six platform areas

| Area | Domain | Products |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations | Behavior & Cognition | Drivers, theories, cognitive biases |
| Methods | Influence & Change | Influence methods and behavior-change approaches |
| Application | Frameworks & Models | Framework and model explorers |
| Assessment | Measurement & Evaluation | Assessment and evaluation methods |
| Inquiry | Research & Discourse | Publications feed, historical case studies, Cognitive Security/podcast mapping |
| Learning | Training & Education | Learning modules, courses, exercises, simulations, games |

The taxonomy of the homepage is not a replacement for the ontology inside any Explorer. The overview's lines convey organizational connections, not empirical or causal relationships.

## Feed categories are a different taxonomy

- Behavioral Science
- Technology & Modeling
- Operations & Strategy
- Application & Analysis

An article can carry multiple tags. Selecting multiple categories shows items from **any** selected category. This is visibly separate from the six platform areas; the word Application occurs in both taxonomies but their roles differ.

The first 24 published selections are loaded with the homepage. Older selections are divided into deterministic static pages and loaded only on request, keeping the initial page bounded as the research database grows.

## Visual system

- Navy `#062f4e`; dark ink `#083b58`; PSYWERX blue `#076a94`.
- Pale blue accents and a light `#f3f6f8` canvas.
- System sans-serif stack; no supplied font binaries or external font dependency.
- Restrained rounded cards, thin borders, generous spacing, no stock photographs.
- Original network-brain mark in the hero and knowledge overview.
- Original wordmark, cropped only to remove unused canvas, in header/footer.
- Original wide banner used as a faded image treatment in the About section.
- Conventional inline SVG UI symbols. They are not replacement PSYWERX logos or purported empirical graphs.

## Purposeful limits

No search or Ask PSYWERX control. No empty fake application destinations. No unsupported launch dates, membership counts, testimonials, endorsements, certification claims, or tax-deductibility claims. No fabricated statistical counts for the evolving Drivers ontology.

The two live tool previews are illustrative interface glyphs; they do not depict or claim particular coded relationships.
