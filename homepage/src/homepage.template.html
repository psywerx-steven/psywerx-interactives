ABOUT PSYWERX

Built to bridge disciplines.
Designed for practical use.

PSYWERX brings behavioral and social science into a shared space for discovery, application, and learning. Our nonprofit mission is to make that knowledge easier to find, understand, and use.

We’re building for researchers, practitioners, and educators across sectors—including those working in national security and the information environment.
