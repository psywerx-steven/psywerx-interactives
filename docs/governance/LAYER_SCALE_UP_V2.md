# Layer Scale-Up V2

## Purpose and authority boundary

Layer Scale-Up V2 is a staged scientific funnel for completing the remaining Layers with less repeated work while preserving the safeguards established by the Psychological Layer. It is process guidance and deterministic tooling. It changes no scientific record, ontology, target semantics, lifecycle state, or activation state.

Human authority remains required for scientific governance and activation. Candidate generation, triage, evidence synthesis, and model review never grant scientific authority. There are no candidate quotas, and a Layer may legitimately produce no governable claim.

## Funnel

### STAGE 0 — mechanical Layer inventory

Freeze the base commit and enumerate every Family, Driver, RDS, existing incident Relationship, prior candidate reference, and cross-Layer dependency. Produce stable IDs and coverage rows. This stage performs no literature inference.

Exit when all entities and existing propositions have one owner and a deterministic coverage route.

### STAGE 1 — Family scientific landscape

For each Family, describe major constructs, plausible mechanisms, known measurement boundaries, likely action/exposure domains, and important null or contrary traditions. Reuse verified source identity and prior Family work. This is bounded landscape work rather than a claim-by-claim exhaustive search.

Exit when every Driver has a landscape-level mechanism assessment and every RDS has an explicit causal-target safeguard.

### STAGE 2 — cheap candidate triage

Triage each coverage route before deep research. Allowed negative outcomes are:

- `NOT_APPLICABLE`
- `NO_PLAUSIBLE_MECHANISM`
- `INSUFFICIENT_PRELIMINARY_SIGNAL`
- `SEARCHED_NO_EXACT_EVIDENCE`

Each outcome needs a concise reason and search/landscape provenance. Negative coverage is not a zero effect and does not become a rejected scientific proposition without the appropriate evidence.

Only a plausible, exact proposition advances. It must name the operation or exposure, target Driver, effect property, direction or state dependence, timing, population/context, and important alternative explanations. Broad Driver × domain × effect-property enumeration does not create a duty to conduct deep research for every cell.

### STAGE 3 — deep evidence for plausible candidates

Search deeply only for propositions that survived Stage 2. Resolve exact construct and manipulation alignment, causal identification, comparator, timing, population transfer, null/contrary evidence, functional form, shared contributions, and duplicate-production-edge risk.

Retain mixed results and stop early when the exact proposition cannot be supported. Do not optimize candidate yield.

### STAGE 4 — sourceFinding and EvidenceAssessment generation

Verify source identity before registration or canonical reuse. Create traceable sourceFindings and one synthesis per assertion. Preserve access depth, preprint/publication identity, shared datasets, review/included-study dependence, theoretical versus experimental roles, nulls, contrary findings, and uncertainty. Evidence confidence is not effect magnitude.

### STAGE 5 — skeptical verification

Independently test the strongest remaining claims, boundary conditions, construct alignment, RDS safety, source overlap, candidate deduplication, and protected science. Downgrade claims when warranted. A `REVIEW_READY` label is a review queue state rather than scientific approval.

### STAGE 6 — human governance package

Compress repeated rows into distinct scientific decisions, grouped low-risk decisions, individual consequential decisions, blockers, and workflow acknowledgements. Keep recommendations, human approval, materialization, and activation as separate durable events.

Materialization follows exact human authorization and remains inactive unless activation is separately authorized. Activation audits are read-only and assess identity, effect, Relationship, and EvidenceAssessment readiness separately.

## Escalation routing

The default route is defined in [Layer Scale-Up V2 model routing](LAYER_SCALE_UP_V2_MODEL_ROUTING.md). Escalation requires a recorded reason and a bounded question. Escalated review returns to the same structured record contracts; model identity never enters scientific schemas.

## Coverage and stopping rules

Every Driver receives coverage accounting. Deep evidence work is required only when a scientifically plausible exact candidate survives triage, an existing proposition needs evidence review, or a consequential ambiguity cannot be assessed from the Family landscape.

Stop a candidate route when its declared negative outcome is supported. Reopen only for a materially different proposition, evidence base, or human instruction. Do not convert `UNKNOWN`, `INSUFFICIENT`, or a non-detection into zero.

## Preserved safeguards

- No candidate quotas or yield target.
- Null and contrary evidence remain first-class findings.
- Cross-Family propositions receive one scientific decision and explicit ownership/consultation metadata.
- RDS remain calculated states and cannot be direct causal-effect targets.
- Sources deduplicate by DOI, PMID, work/version, dataset, review inclusion, and semantic role.
- Existing production science is protected by object comparison against the frozen baseline.
- Governance, inactive materialization, activation, practitioner actionability, and quantitative execution remain separate.
- Architecture, ontology, definition, classification, alias, and crosswalk questions route to blockers rather than silent implementation.

## Required artifacts

Each Layer produces a frozen inventory, Family coverage ledgers, triage outcomes, exact candidates, source findings, EvidenceAssessments, skeptical-review results, cross-Family issues, architecture escalations, governance index, compressed recommendation package, protected-science report, and validation manifest. Empty candidate sets are valid when coverage is complete.

## Psychological read-only benchmark

The deterministic benchmark in `scripts/layer_scale_up_v2.py` reads the completed Psychological structured package without regenerating it. It must surface:

- all 14 Families and all 135 entities;
- 134 Drivers and the sole RDS, `PSY-078`;
- all 111 existing Relationships reviewed;
- one Relationship candidate, 29 HappeningTypes, 30 EffectAssertions, and 31 EvidenceAssessments;
- 45 lifecycle-bearing `REVIEW_READY` records and all high-priority governance rows;
- all three preserved blockers; and
- all 64 source-overlap/alignment issues.

The Psychological package shows where work accumulated: Family landscapes and source extraction, exact candidate evidence alignment, and cross-Family/source deduplication. V2 avoids mandatory deep searches for routes that fail cheap triage and avoids extracting the same source before a proposition survives. Human workload follows compressed decision units instead of raw ledger rows.

No precise token or credit savings are claimed. The benchmark reports mechanically observable counts and qualitative workload changes because the repository does not contain reliable per-stage model-usage telemetry.

## Branch and review policy

Process changes must not modify `data/`, scientific schemas, or governed scientific records. CI validates the benchmark, required safeguards, templates, and allowed diff scope. The workflow can be merged through normal process review when those gates pass.
