learning and trust reviews a shared construct-boundary register. No clear
dependency justifies reordering. Cross-Family issues remain centrally identified
and are revisited when the other Family is processed.

scientific IDs, never sums of incident counts. Directed scientific ownership

## Research and safety

Hierarchical Family landscape research is followed by exact construct checks and
candidate-specific verification. Access depth and search gaps remain explicit;
this is not fourteen formal systematic reviews. Reuse exact sources and actions,
not source-wide efficacy. Register review/component overlap without independent
replication inflation. Preserve null/contrary results and uncertain direction.

State/trait, within/between-person, construct/measure, shared items/methods,
manipulation alignment, temporal order, population/task transfer, invariance,
moderation and mediation are required review lenses. The Psychological RDS must
be identified mechanically and reviewed under D10; no direct RDS effect target.

All science stays CANDIDATE, RESEARCH_NEEDED or REVIEW_READY plus NOT_ELIGIBLE.
Blocks are orthogonal NEEDS_GOVERNANCE_INPUT. No source registration, ontology
repair, prior pilot changes, production mutation, quantitative execution,
recommendations, other-Layer Family audit, or deployment is permitted.
