### CONTROL_THREAT — SRC200

SELECTED_FULL_TEXT; EXPERIMENTAL; SUPPORTS.

Methods/manipulation checks; https://doi.org/10.1080/03637750500111815

Higher-control scripts increased measured freedom threat in both topics. Script wording also changes derogation/content; not isolated one-word or independently randomized latent state.

Exact specified message package and immediate subjective appraisal only; assignment method ambiguity, convenience samples and bundled contrasts; no causal mediator or universal behavior benefit.

### PLOHL_THREAT — SRC-CAND-PSY-LAYER-0156

SELECTED_FULL_TEXT; EXPERIMENTAL; SUPPORTS.

Methods/results; https://doi.org/10.1080/17538068.2023.2279395

Perceived freedom threat higher for high-control messages in both topics. Downstream attitudes/intention nondetection in high-trust COVID group despite reactance, not automatic advocacy effect.

Bundle not pure lexical control. Science trust measured and dichotomized, not randomized exact PSY-113 moderator; no calibration or universal effect.
