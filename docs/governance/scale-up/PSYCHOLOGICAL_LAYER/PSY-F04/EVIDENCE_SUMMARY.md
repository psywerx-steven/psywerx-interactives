### HOMEWORK_INTEREST — SRC-CAND-PSY-LAYER-0029

SELECTED_FULL_TEXT; EXPERIMENTAL; SUPPORTS.

https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/2010_PatallCooperWynn_JEP.pdf pp.899–904 and 913: Participants, Procedure, IMI, Analysis 1 hypothesis testing, limitations.

Students reported higher homework interest/enjoyment under choice. This is not proof that increased perceived autonomy independently mediated the effect.

Missingness particularly limits lower-achieving-student transfer; participants anticipated crossover; only 14 classes and limited duration. Same-sample Analysis 2 correlational, not pathway evidence.

### PUZZLE_OPTION_NULL — SRC-CAND-PSY-LAYER-0030

SELECTED_FULL_TEXT; EXPERIMENTAL; NULL_FINDING.

https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2003_ReeveNixHamm_JEP.pdf pp.381–382: Study 2 Method and Discussion.

Perceived choice changed, but intrinsic motivation, internal locus and volition did not detectably differ. This constrains transfer beyond the homework scope.

OUTSIDE high-school-homework scope, mixed measurement index, postassignment solver exclusion and selected accessed sections. Nonsignificance is not equivalence. Already included in 2008 review.

### EA-CAND-PSY-LAYER-0007

MIXED / MODERATE / MODERATE — REVIEW_READY + NOT_ELIGIBLE.

Providing two content-aligned, intermediate-difficulty homework options rather than assigning a yoked option can increase unit-end self-reported INTEREST/ENJOYMENT OF THAT HOMEWORK among grades 9–12 students in comparable short, teacher-supervised instructional units. No universal all-task, culture, age, trait or long-term effect.

Within-class randomization and yoking support a scoped total operation effect. Contextual synthesis is heterogeneous; puzzle option-choice null constrains transfer and overlaps the review. Other homework outcomes remain nondetected. No numerical estimate or independent-replication count is inferred.
