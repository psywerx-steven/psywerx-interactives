### EA-CAND-PSY-LAYER-0005

SUPPORTS / MODERATE / MODERATE — REVIEW_READY + NOT_ELIGIBLE.

In the accessed young-adult virtual ball-game protocols, stopping passes after initial inclusion can increase immediate feelings of being ignored/excluded compared with continued inclusion. The claim is about perceived exclusion DURING THAT GAME, not chronic rejection, actual friendship loss, physiological stress or future behavior. Age-six non-detection is explicitly outside this proposed adult scope.

Two independent publications provide aligned young-adult contrasts; one uses pseudo-random allocation and the other a narrow randomized student sample. The age-six null remains a transport limitation. No pooled effect size or universal effect is inferred.
