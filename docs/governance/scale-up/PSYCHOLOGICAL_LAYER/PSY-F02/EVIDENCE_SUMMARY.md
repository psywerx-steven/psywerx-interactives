### EA-CAND-PSY-LAYER-0003

MIXED / LIMITED / MODERATE — REVIEW_READY + NOT_ELIGIBLE.

In the specified healthy young-adult aversive-noise task, a response-contingent termination protocol versus its pseudo-yoked noncontingent comparator increased perceived control OVER THAT TASK. Not a universal effect of control availability, not specifically a validated measure of stressor controllability, not chronic agency or psychological safety.

Randomized protocol preceded task-specific control ratings; initial-study results support task-control distinction while mixed downstream readouts remain exclusions, not efficacy claims. Limited strength reflects one publication and narrow population.
