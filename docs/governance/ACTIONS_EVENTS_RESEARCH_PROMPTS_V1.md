# Governed Actions & Events research prompts V1

Architecture authority: [AE01–AE12](ACTIONS_EVENTS_V1_GOVERNANCE_DECISION.md).
These five complete prompts are future workflow instructions, not authorization
to begin any Family audit or scientific population now. Supply separate explicit
human scope authorization before using one. Each includes its own complete recall
checklist so it can be copied independently.

## 1. Family Relationship Audit

```text
TASK: Perform only the separately authorized Relationship audit for [FAMILY_ID].
INPUTS: explicit scope authorization; frozen commit and audit ID; current entity,
Family, Relationship, evidence and governance schemas; source-register hash;
entity/alias/crosswalk snapshots; RDS derivations; legacy and native records;
candidate lineage; previous decisions/rejections; existing shared issue queue.
The complete search checklist is included below.

1. Mechanically reconcile members, types, definitions and incident IDs. Preserve
   V3 authority separately from V1 completeness/executability and count a V3
   projection once. Trace RDS inputs, shared denominators, calculation windows,
   external parameters and exogenous-root/double-count risk without retyping.
2. Review all current internal, same-Layer cross-Family and cross-Layer incoming
   and outgoing assertions before proposing gaps. Record exact proposition,
   endpoints, relationFamily/predicate, polarity/shape, mechanism, claim role,
   scope, timing, evidence/conflicts and audit disposition. A revision is a
   proposal with field changes and identity implications, not a canonical edit.
3. Search plausible gaps using definitions/mechanisms and primary scientific
   literature. Triage causal, association, temporal, moderation, derivational,
   compositional, realization, semantic and pathway meanings separately. Reject
   tautologies, formula-induced causality and duplicate constituent propagation.
   Graph reachability is not demonstrated mediation. Apply existing D10 gates.
4. Apply ownership: source/subject Family for directed records, RDS Family for
   derivation, lexically lower Family for symmetric records, with all affected
   Families consulted. Deduplicate scoped propositions, aliases and legacy
   projections. Use one shared cross-Family issue rather than duplicate edges.
5. Keep useful action/event evidence in a linked Pass B inbox; do not infer all
   downstream effects from it. Record reviewed-section readiness explicitly.

OUTPUTS: frozen manifest; full incident-record disposition table; candidate or
revision packet only where authorized; source-level evidence/findings and search
log; duplicate/rejection registry; cross-Family handoffs; RDS and graph flags;
readiness report; governance questions. Record read access as full text,
abstract, documentation or metadata. Retain durable IDs/passages, design,
population, exposure/comparator/outcome, confounder controls, timing,
limitations, contrary/null findings and shared datasets. Do not invent precision.

AUTOMATION: read, inventory, deduplicate, research, structure, validate, and use
only authorized non-governed lifecycle transitions; NOT_ELIGIBLE throughout.
HUMAN BOUNDARY: canonical proposition/reclassification/source registration,
governance/activation and substantive revisions require their own authorization.
COMPLETION: every member/incident record has a disposition or explicit deferral;
all search cells have status; references resolve or access limits are recorded;
all candidates have owner/lineage/evidence gaps; no production state changed.
NO FINDINGS: report adequate search with no supported candidate as a valid
result; retain query/date/access limits. Do not fill degree gaps mechanically.
UNRESOLVED: record issue ID, affected claims, owner, missing evidence and next
step; dependent claims stay RESEARCH_NEEDED or NEEDS_GOVERNANCE_INPUT.

Inspect origin Layers independently from target Layers:
Biological; Psychological; Social; Cultural; Physical/Environmental;
Institutional/Structural; Informational; Technological.
Allow multiple origin Layer tags and record actor/source system separately.

Search overlapping domains, retaining NOT_APPLICABLE with reasons where needed:
A deliberate human interventions (training, communication, policy, incentives,
  treatment, design, allocation, constraints);
B routine/unintended human activity (ordinary behavior, accidents, side effects);
C external events/shocks (disasters, attacks, scandals, elections, disruption,
  pandemic and leadership change);
D environmental exposures (heat, noise, crowding, pollution, scarcity,
  sustained conditions);
E institutional/structural change (laws, restructuring, sanctions, censorship,
  market rules and authority);
F technological change (platform/algorithm change, outages, automation,
  capabilities);
G social/network events/processes (formation, migration, protest, leader loss,
  ties forming/dissolving);
H informational exposures (rumor, disclosure, news, propaganda, repeated
  messages and information availability);
I biological/physiological events/processes (illness, injury, intoxication,
  sleep loss, development and aging).
Do not classify these as mutually exclusive. External is relative to a named
system boundary; it does not imply random, unconfounded or causally exogenous.
Search discrete, repeated, continuous, gradual, cumulative and cyclic patterns.

For each plausible effect ask what property changes and how it changes:
1 LEVEL: increase/decrease.
2 VARIABILITY: stability, fluctuation, variance, predictability;
  stabilize/destabilize or increase/decrease variation.
3 RATE: speed up/slow down change.
4 THRESHOLD: raise/lower an activation point.
5 TIMING: earlier/later onset; changed lag, duration or recovery.
6 PERSISTENCE: lasting effects, accumulation, carryover, decay, reversibility.
7 RELATIONSHIP_STRENGTH: amplify, attenuate, buffer, suppress.
8 RELATIONSHIP_DIRECTION: reverse only under specified conditions.
9 ENABLEMENT: permit/prevent a mechanism, opportunity or pathway operating.
10 FUNCTIONAL_SHAPE: linear, threshold, saturation, U/inverted-U, cyclic,
   other non-monotonic or unknown; never force a universal sign.
11 STRUCTURE: resources, opportunities, constraints, access, real-world ties
   or configuration. Changing a real-world network does not edit ontology edges.
Also inspect reach/distribution, subgroup heterogeneity, synergy/antagonism,
intended versus observed direction, unintended consequences, and beneficial,
harmful, mixed and null outcomes relative to an explicit stakeholder/value.

For each search cell record one: NOT_INVESTIGATED, NOT_APPLICABLE,
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, SUPPORTED_EFFECT, or SUPPORTED_NULL, with rationale.
These are governed research-log outcomes, not scientific relationship types.
Unknown is not zero. Nonsignificance alone is not evidence of equivalence/null.

EIGHT SEPARATE ASSERTION DIMENSIONS:
A claim semantics (causal, association, temporal, moderation, derivational,
compositional, realization, semantic; pathway remains a separate governed object);
B evidence basis (experimental, quasi/natural experiment, longitudinal observation,
cross-sectional observation, qualitative, historical/case, synthesis, theoretical/
mechanistic, definitional/calculational, model/simulation, expert judgment,
untested hypothesis), allowing multiple bases and shared datasets;
C production method (source extraction, synthesis, model/pathway inference,
proposed hypothesis); AI inference is never empirical observation;
D disposition; E reasoned confidence; F clarity/completeness of target, direction,
mechanism, population, context, timing, measurement and boundaries;
G provenance (canonical identifiers, passages/access depth, roles, revision);
H governance/activation and separate scientific/model/practitioner eligibility.
Preserve supportive, mixed, null and contrary sourceFindings BEFORE synthesis.
No evidence grade or confidence becomes a graph weight. Approval is not certainty.
Practitioner-use review includes actor control, prerequisites, feasibility,
legal constraints, ethical/risk constraints and population/context applicability.
UNKNOWN never means zero; nonsignificance alone does not demonstrate null.
```
