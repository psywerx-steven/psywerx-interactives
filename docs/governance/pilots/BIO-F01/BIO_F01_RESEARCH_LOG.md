# BIO-F01 structured evidence search log

**Method:** Structured evidence search/audit, not a formal systematic review

**Search date:** 2026-09-05
**Audit ID:** `AUD-BIO-F01-RI-V1-20260905-001`

## Scope and systems

The audit searched the governed PSYWERX source register and PubMed-indexed
literature surfaced through scholarly web search. Searches prioritized primary
human experiments, randomized/quasi-experimental studies, systematic reviews,
meta-analyses, and authoritative evidence syntheses. Search concepts combined
canonical Driver/RDS names and close scientific terms with causal,
longitudinal, experimental, moderation, mediation, and intervention terms.

Principal concept groups were:

- sleep duration/restriction/extension × fatigue, cognition, cognitive load,
  sleep inertia, recovery, intervention;
- sleep fragmentation/continuity × daytime fatigue, performance, pain, noise,
  CBT-I, acoustic attenuation;
- circadian phase/chronotype/alignment × DLMO, forced desynchrony, sleep dose,
  light, melatonin, moderation;
- sleep inertia × prior sleep, biological night, nap, caffeine, light,
  countermeasure; and
- scheduling × school start, shift rotation, protected rest, sleep duration.

Title/abstract screening excluded animal-only evidence, purely diagnostic
papers with no relevant relationship, studies whose “sleep quality” construct
could not be mapped to duration or continuity, non-scientific advice, and
papers duplicating a more comprehensive included synthesis. Narrative mentions
were used for retrieval only. Full risk-of-bias re-adjudication and exhaustive
database deduplication were out of scope; therefore this is not called a
systematic review.

## Inclusion and exclusion notes

- A 2024 component network meta-analysis of CBT-I was read as corroboration,
  but the package candidate does not claim that only two components exist.
- ICU acoustic-intervention reviews were included for a context-specific
  effect; they were not generalized to every home, workplace, or hospital.
- CPAP literature was screened but not retained as a generic Sleep Continuity
  intervention because treatment targets obstructive sleep apnea etiology and
  requires a narrower clinical assertion.
- Nap studies were reviewed but no short-nap InterventionEffect was created:
  short naps can both counter sleepiness and produce inertia, with timing and
  prior sleep creating mixed results.
- Post-awakening auditory alarm studies were not promoted because evidence is
  heterogeneous and population/task specific.
- Studies using broad “sleep quality” without separable duration, continuity,
  or phase constructs informed uncertainty but did not alone create edges.
- Two systematic pain reviews partly overlap underlying cohorts; they are not
  treated as fully independent replications.

## Unresolved evidence gaps

- Direct continuity-specific interventional evidence for a causal effect on
  persistent pain burden.
- Outcome alignment between vigilance performance and the ontology’s
  Cognitive Fatigue / Cognitive Load constructs.
- Pathway-specific mediation studies aligning canonical noise, continuity, and
  fatigue constructs.
- Generalizable objective-performance evidence for post-awakening light.
- High-certainty schedule-intervention evidence across shift systems.
- Canonical source registration for all supplemental sources before any
  candidate could be governed and activated.
