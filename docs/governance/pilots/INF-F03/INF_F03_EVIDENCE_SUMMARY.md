## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-001

[Poor writing, not specialized concepts, drives processing difficulty in legal language](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010027722000580) — Eric Martínez; Francis Mollica; Edward Gibson; 2022; Cognition. DOI: 10.1016/j.cognition.2022.105070. Access: SELECTED_FULL_TEXT, SELECTED_FULL_TEXT: experimental summary and discussion.

Editing surface features is separable from changing legal concepts. Benefits differ by feature; passive voice is not equivalent to center embedding. Corpus plus experiment; not an estimate for all audiences or all dimensions; no direct cognitive-load measure extracted.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-002

[The effects of communicating uncertainty on public trust in facts and numbers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7149229/) — Anne Marthe van der Bles; Sander van der Linden; Alexandra L. J. Freeman; David J. Spiegelhalter; 2020; PNAS. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1913678117. Access: SELECTED_FULL_TEXT, Discussion and experimental overview.

Numeric ranges generally produced little source-trust reduction; verbal expressions sometimes reduced trust. Outcome-specific and format-dependent. Do not interpret source trust as self-confidence calibration; studies within one research program are not five independent replications.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-003

[The effects of communicating uncertainty around statistics, on public trust](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38026007/) — John Kerr; Anne-Marthe van der Bles; Sarah Dryhurst; Claudia R. Schneider; Vivien Chopurian; Alexandra L. J. Freeman; Sander van der Linden; 2023; Royal Society Open Science. DOI: 10.1098/rsos.230604. Access: ABSTRACT, Abstract.

Numeric uncertainty had minimal source-trust impact; unquantified verbal statements could reduce number and source trust. Present/projected statistics differ; no transferable effect size; preprint and publication represent the same studies.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-004

[Working memory constraints on the processing of syntactic ambiguity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1537232/) — Maryellen C. MacDonald; Marcel A. Just; Patricia A. Carpenter; 1992; Cognitive Psychology. DOI: 10.1016/0010-0285(92)90003-K. Access: ABSTRACT, Abstract.

Additional processing followed ambiguity; capacity-related patterns motivate a bounded processing-demand hypothesis. Trait reading span is not current Working Memory Availability; reading time is not a direct occupied-capacity measurement.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-005

[Underspecification of syntactic ambiguities: Evidence from self-paced reading](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18323075/) — Benjamin Swets; Timothy Desmet; Charles Clifton Jr.; Fernanda Ferreira; 2008; Memory & Cognition. DOI: 10.3758/MC.36.1.201. Access: ABSTRACT, Abstract.

Ambiguity advantage appeared with superficial questions and was attenuated by interpretation demands. Contradicts a universal ambiguity-processing-cost claim, not every bounded ambiguity effect; performance proxy is task dependent.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-006

[Noise-induced differences in the complexity of spoken language](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/17470218221124869) — Catherine T. Pham; Elisabeth A. Karuza; 2023; Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology; online 2022. DOI: 10.1177/17470218221124869. Access: SELECTED_FULL_TEXT, pp.1609,1617–1618; Table 4.

Speech quantity decreased; mean T-unit length and clausal density did not show significant condition effects. Quantity is not the full complexity construct; remote sound calibration and sensitivity exclusions limit transfer. Nonsignificance is not equivalence.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-007

[Do Text Simplification Systems Preserve Meaning? A Human Evaluation via Reading Comprehension](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.24/) — Sweta Agrawal; Marine Carpuat; 2024; Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics. DOI: 10.1162/tacl_a_00653. Access: ABSTRACT, Abstract.

Even the strongest supervised system left at least 14% of questions unanswerable from simplified text. This is information-loss evidence, not proof of a uniform complexity reduction; systems/corpora are not universal.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-008

[Getting through: communicating complex information](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/working-paper/2023/getting-through-communicating-complex-information.pdf) — Michael McMahon; Matthew Naylor; 2023; Bank of England Staff Working Paper 1047. DOI: not verified; durable URL retained. Access: SELECTED_FULL_TEXT, Abstract; introduction; sections 4–5; current hosted version.

Conceptual complexity mattered more than syntactic complexity; syntactic effects were small/null in these materials. Working-paper/current hosted revision; CCI is a specific operationalization, not canonical INF-013; threshold not portable.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-009

[Sleep deprivation and its effects on communication during individual and collaborative tasks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30816244/) — Benjamin C. Holding; Tina Sundelin; Mats Lekander; John Axelsson; 2019; Scientific Reports. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-019-39271-6. Access: ABSTRACT, Abstract.

Builder deprivation impaired model-building but instruction-giver deprivation predicted improvement; other reported speech/task measures were unaffected. Role-dependent performance is not direct message ambiguity or surface-complexity coding; no recommendation to deprive sleep.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-010

[To err is human: patient misinterpretations of prescription drug label instructions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17587533/) — Michael S. Wolf; Terry C. Davis; William Shrank; David N. Rapp; Pat F. Bass; Ulla M. Connor; Marla Clayman; Ruth M. Parker; 2007; Patient Education and Counseling. DOI: 10.1016/j.pec.2007.03.024. Access: ABSTRACT, Abstract.

Language, complexity, implicit intervals, distractors, familiarity and attention contributed to misinterpretation themes. Diagnostic qualitative evidence does not estimate a causal rewrite effect; any instruction revision requires clinical accuracy review.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-011

[Improving communication of uncertainty in the reports of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change](https://research.library.fordham.edu/psych_facultypubs/87/) — David V. Budescu; Stephen Broomell; Han-Hui Por; 2009; Psychological Science. DOI: not verified; durable URL retained. Access: ABSTRACT, Institutional author abstract.

Interpretations deviated from guidelines even with access to them; an alternative communication format was evaluated. Instrument-specific dispersion is not automatically objective message ambiguity; full methods not accessed.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-012

[Are Good Texts Always Better? Interactions of Text Coherence, Background Knowledge, and Levels of Understanding in Learning From Text](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1207/s1532690xci1401_1) — Danielle S. McNamara; Eileen Kintsch; Nancy Butler Songer; Walter Kintsch; 1996; Cognition and Instruction. DOI: 10.1207/s1532690xci1401_1. Access: ABSTRACT, Publisher abstract/search extract.

Text coherence and background knowledge interact in learning; this does not establish a PSYWERX pathway. Access was limited; recover exact methods before a mediation/moderation assertion; cohesion is derived.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-013

[A Meta-Analysis Synthesizing the Effects of Three Uncertainty Types in Science Communication](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10755470251314129) — Zhuo Guo; Ke Liu; Meng Chen; 2025; Science Communication. DOI: 10.1177/10755470251314129. Access: ABSTRACT, Publisher abstract and included-reference markers.

Uncertainty types differed; credibility and attitude effects were not interchangeable; cultural avoidance/topic/text length were reported moderators. Full text restricted; study-level moderator causality not established; includes primary literature and is not independent replication.

## SRC-CAND-INF-F03-014

[Social Network Limits Language Complexity](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/cogs.12683) — Matthew Lou-Magnuson; Luca Onnis; 2018; Cognitive Science. DOI: 10.1111/cogs.12683. Access: SELECTED_FULL_TEXT, Methods search extract.

Model concerns language-system evolution, not an observed manipulation of a particular message. Do not label model inference empirical or equate language-system complexity with INF-077.
